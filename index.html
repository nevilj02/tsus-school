Privacy Policy

App: TSUS School Bus
Operator: The Shri Ram Universal School Gandhidham
Effective date: 15 July 2026
Last updated: 15 July 2026

The Shri Ram Universal School Gandhidham (“School”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) operates the TSUS School Bus mobile application (the “App”) and related backend services. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, and protect information when you use the App on Android (Google Play) or related platforms.

By using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to users of the App, including:

  • Parents / guardians
  • Students (where accounts are created by the School)
  • Drivers and conductors
  • Teachers and school administrators

The App is intended for school transport management for students of The Shri Ram Universal School Gandhidham. Student accounts and information are managed under the authority of the School and parents/guardians.

2. Information we collect

Depending on your role and how you use the App, we may collect:

2.1 Account and identity information

  • Name (first, middle, last)
  • Email address
  • Mobile and alternate mobile numbers
  • Gender and date of birth
  • Address, city, state, and pincode
  • Emergency contact name and phone number
  • Profile photograph (if uploaded)
  • Login credentials (username/code or email, and password)

2.2 Student and school information

  • Admission number, grade/class, and academic details
  • Assigned bus, route, and pickup point
  • Parent/guardian linkage (e.g., matching contact numbers)
  • Attendance / boarding-related records associated with bus trips

2.3 Device and app information

  • Device ID and device name
  • Platform (Android/iOS), OS version, and app version
  • Push notification tokens needed to deliver alerts

2.4 Location information (school bus tracking)

  • Real-time and historical location of school buses collected from vehicle GPS / tracking systems (not typically from your phone’s GPS)
  • Bus-related data such as speed, ignition status, direction, and approximate address derived from coordinates
  • School and pickup-point coordinates used for routes and alerts

Parents, staff, and authorized users may see live bus location and related alerts (for example, bus started or bus approaching) to support student transport safety.

2.5 Photos and documents

  • User profile photos
  • Vehicle-related documents/photos (e.g., registration, insurance) when uploaded by authorized staff

2.6 Notifications and usage within the App

  • Notification content and delivery records (title, description, type, sender/receiver)
  • Authentication session data (access/refresh tokens) for keeping you signed in securely

3. How we collect information

  • Directly from you or school administrators when accounts are created or updated
  • When you sign in and use App features
  • From school systems and devices used for transport operations (including vehicle GPS trackers and attendance/biometric systems where configured)
  • Automatically from your device for basic technical and notification delivery purposes

4. How we use information

We use information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts and roles
  • Operate school bus routes, pickup points, and live vehicle tracking
  • Notify parents/guardians and staff about bus status, boarding, pickup/drop events, and other transport alerts
  • Record and display student transport / attendance-related events
  • Authenticate users and protect accounts
  • Support school administration, reporting, and operations
  • Maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the App and related services
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

5. Children’s information

The App processes information about school students, including minors, solely for school transport and related educational administration purposes. Student data is created and managed by the School (and where applicable, linked parent/guardian contacts). We do not knowingly sell children’s personal information or use it for advertising/marketing profiles.

Parents or guardians who have questions about a child’s data should contact the School using the details below.

6. Sharing of information

We do not sell personal information. We may share information only as needed to operate the App and school transport services, including with:

  • Authorized school staff (admins, teachers, drivers, conductors) according to their role
  • Parents/guardians linked to a student, for bus location, alerts, and transport status
  • Service providers that help us run the App, such as:
    • Cloud hosting and database infrastructure
    • Firebase Cloud Messaging (push notifications)
    • Cloud image/file storage providers (e.g., Cloudinary)
    • Mapping/geocoding providers (e.g., Google Maps / OpenStreetMap services) for addresses, distances, and map-related features
    • Vehicle GPS / tracking vendors used for bus location data
    • Attendance/biometric device providers where configured by the School
  • Authorities when required by law, court order, or to protect safety and security

Service providers are expected to process data only to provide services to us and in accordance with applicable data-protection requirements.

7. Permissions the App may request

Depending on platform and features enabled, the App may request permissions such as:

  • Notifications — to send bus and school transport alerts
  • Internet / network access — to communicate with our servers
  • Camera / photos / storage — only if you upload a profile photo or authorized staff upload documents

Bus tracking shown in the App is primarily based on vehicle GPS devices, not continuous tracking of the user’s personal phone location. If a future App version requests device location permission, it will be used only for optional features (for example showing your position relative to a pickup point) and disclosed at the time of the request.

8. Data retention

We retain personal information for as long as needed to provide school transport services, maintain operational and safety records, meet academic/administrative needs, resolve disputes, and comply with legal obligations. When information is no longer required, we take reasonable steps to delete, anonymize, or securely archive it according to School policy.

9. Data security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect personal information, including encrypted password storage and authenticated access controls. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law and school policy, you may:

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion or restriction of processing where legally permitted (noting that some student/transport records may need to be retained by the School)
  • Disable push notifications in device settings
  • Request account deactivation through the School

To exercise these rights, contact us using the details in Section 13.

11. International / third-country processing

Some service providers (for example cloud notification, storage, or mapping providers) may process data on servers located outside India. Where this occurs, we take reasonable steps to ensure appropriate safeguards consistent with applicable law.

12. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy, to the extent permitted by law.

14. Google Play Data safety summary

For Google Play Console “Data safety” disclosures, the App may collect and process data in these categories (purposes: app functionality, account management, and school transport communications):

  • Name, email, phone number, address
  • Photos (profile / authorized document uploads)
  • Other user info (student school details, emergency contacts)
  • Device or other IDs
  • Location (bus GPS location shared with authorized users; not sold)
  • App interactions related to notifications and transport status

Data is not sold. Data may be shared with school-authorized roles and the service providers listed above. The App handles children’s data as part of school services. Encryption in transit is used for API communications when served over HTTPS.
